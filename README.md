# TrackMason AI (beta)

**By Washburne Audio Labs.** TrackMason AI helps musicians finish songs: start
from a riff, a recording or a whole multitrack, and every tool works on that
song. It finds the tempo, key and chords, writes drums, chords and melodies that
play along with you, helps with lyrics, and checks your mix and master.

Everything runs on your own computer. Your songs and lyrics are never uploaded.

## Installing on Windows

1. Open the downloaded installer. Windows may say **"Windows protected your PC"**
   because the installer isn't signed yet: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
2. Keep **Install the free AI songwriter** ticked. The installer downloads the AI
   engine (about 1.7 GB), so give it a few minutes.
3. TrackMason opens when it's done. The first time, it downloads its AI model in
   the background (2 to 5 GB); the sidebar shows the progress, and every other
   tool works meanwhile.

## Installing on a Mac

1. Open the downloaded `.dmg` and drag **TrackMason AI** into **Applications**.
2. Open it from Applications. macOS says it can't verify the app (this beta isn't
   notarized yet): click **Done**, then go to **System Settings > Privacy &
   Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
3. If asked, allow access to your **Documents** folder (your songs are kept in
   Documents > TrackMason AI). TrackMason then sets up the AI songwriter by itself.
